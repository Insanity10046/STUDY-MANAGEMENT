## Fixed Rules (reference — do not edit)

- 25-min timer covers Prime → Null-check only. Hard stop.
- Prime = one sentence. Increase = one sentence. No exceptions.
- Render and Null-check are done with the source closed.
- No re-reading inside SPRINT. Re-reading belongs to DEEP.
- Triage is recorded before DEEP begins. No DEEP without a logged route.
- DEEP is never run in a single sitting.
- The Seal compares against the *same* gap map produced in §1.
