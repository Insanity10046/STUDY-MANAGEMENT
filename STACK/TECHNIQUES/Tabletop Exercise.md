A discussion-based, low tech simulation where learners work through realistic fictional scenario (e.g criminal operation, law enforcement mock investigation) using written materials, diagrams and group conversation--no field work involved.

# THE MECHANISM
Participants receive "injects" (pieces of unfolding information) and must make decisions, apply procedures and justify their reasoning as the scenario progresses. When presented as a text-based game with branching choices, it becomes a **brancing scenario simulation**.

# PURPOSE
To sharpen applied thinking on paper(e.g practising investigative mindsets, law enforcement procedure, and forensic reasoning in a safe, controlled environment)

## GOAL
Develop decision-making skills, reinforce correct procedure, reveal knowledge gaps, and build confidence through guided reflection and discussion.
