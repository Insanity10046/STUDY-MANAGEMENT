# Debrief Session
**Key idea**: 
> Use the debrief as an active recall loop — first dump what you know, then explain it simply, then question it deeply. This exposes gaps and links the new topic to what you already understand.
---
**Key Techniques**:
- **Feynman Technique**: gradually understand and explain the topic in your own words.
- **Blurt / Brain Dump**: dump everything you remember from memory before checking your notes.
- **Socratic Method**: use deep questioning to uncover connections, assumptions, and gaps.
