a **study stack** is your personal combination of tools, techniques, and routines for learning. Like a “tech stack,” but for studying.

It usually includes things like:

- **Tools/apps**: Anki, Notion, Obsidian, Quizlet, Forest, etc.
- **Methods**: active recall, spaced repetition, Pomodoro, Feynman technique
- **Resources**: textbooks, YouTube channels, ChatGPT, past papers
- **Habits/environment**: study schedule, noise level, music/no music, solo vs group study

So a study stack might look like:

> **Anki + Notion + Pomodoro + active recall + past papers**

Basically, it’s the tools, methods, and resources you use in the study system.
