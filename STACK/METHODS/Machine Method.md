# Machine Method
The main idea of this learning system is to have the summary of all the topics of the subject, and just aggressively actively learn the topics using the summaries as the source of information regarding the topics, be it aggressive problem solving, or gathering more information to build and refine the summary. After you master the topics only then would u write them and take notes of them based on what u understood, this way u have a memory storage(the notebook) and when years or months passes by and u forgot certain topics of it u can just take a look at the notes(imitating muscle memory but instead of muscle, its the brain).
### Rule:
- Create summaries of all the topics of the subject 
- Before going to learn all of them, filter the summaries by comparing the informations and see if it captures all the necessary information needed to know in the topic, be it from experts or textbooks. **Make sure you don't waste time in this step, the point is, you just have to make a good summary of all the topics for comprehension and as a scaffold. The refinement of the summary comes later.**
- Aggressively learn the topics of the subject from the summaries, you can gather more information regarding the topic if you get the sunmary and when there's more to the summary or there is something wrong with the summary, refine it to make it more accurate
- If theres any refined summary, relearn the topic again to mastery
- After you master the topic, write the topic in the notebook regarding what u think about the topic and the important things behind it(memory storage)
- After you master all the topics of the subject, do a culminated combination of topics problem of the subject, and analyse weakpoints
- Relearn the gap
