## SQ3R-Flex

| Step | What you do | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **S — Survey** | Headings, bold terms, figures, summaries. Build a map and decide what to skip. | Very fast / scanning |
| **Q — Question** | Turn headings into questions. | Normal |
| **R1 — Read** | Use the right rate tier below. | Variable |
| **R2 — Recite** | Close the text and answer your questions from memory. | Effortful, not timed |
| **R3 — Review** | Re-test later without looking, then check. | Spaced over days |

### R1 Rate Tiers

| Tier | Rate (rough guide) | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Deep encoding** | ~50–250 WPM | Core argument, mechanisms, difficult passages that answer your questions |
| **B — Strategic skimming** | ~350–800 WPM | Context, examples, supporting detail you need to recognize, not master |
| **C — Scanning** | ~1000+ WPM | Locating a specific fact, name, definition, or statistic |

**Rule:** Speed is for triage and location. Slowness is for encoding. Retrieval is for durability.

The WPM numbers are rough relative labels, not targets — text difficulty and your familiarity change them.
