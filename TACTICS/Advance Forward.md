# Advance Forward
**The idea**: 
> Learn the advanced topic, and when you understand it, debrief using the Feynman Technique. If there’s something you need to learn (prerequisites) to learn the advanced topic properly, go back to learn those and then debrief after to find the connections of it to the advanced topic.
---
# Techniques utilised
- **Just-in-Time Support**: Learn the advanced topic now, and only look up the specific prerequisite knowledge you need, exactly when you need it. This prevents getting stuck in beginner basics.
- **Feynman Technique**: Constantly test your understanding by trying to explain a concept in your own words or by using it to solve a real problem.
