CWWR (Crawl-Walk-Walk-Run) is a progressive training pipeline that moves from abstract tabletop reasoning (Crawl), through digital simulation (Walk 1) and sterile physical rehearsal (Walk 2), to full live-environment execution (Run), gradually layering complexity to prevent cognitive overload.

---

**CWWR Protocol (The 4 Phases):**

1. **Crawl (e.g TTX):** Example is Whiteboard/tabletop. Build the logic, rules, and decision-trees. No movement.
2. **Walk-Cognitive (e.g Video Game/Sim):** Example is Digital sandbox. Solve spatial geometry, timing, and sequencing. Zero cost, zero risk.
3. **Walk-Physical (e.g Physical/Mock Scene):** Sterile, controlled physical environment. Train **body mechanics** (gait, positioning, tool handling) *without* live stressors.
4. **Run (Full-Field Exercise):** Live environment with role-players, real weather, and unpredictability. The final integration test.

---

**Rule of Thumb for Partial Layering (Multi-Skill Ops):**

- **Do NOT layer all 5 skills at once until Phase 4 (Run).**
- **Phase 1 (Crawl):** 1–2 skills max. Build foundations.
- **Phase 2 (Video Game):** 2–3 skills max. Solve spatial + procedural.
- **Phase 3 (Physical Walk):** 2–3 skills max. Solve body mechanics + environment.
- **Phase 4 (Run):** **Now** layer all 4–5 skills simultaneously.

**The Golden Rule:** If they fail in the Run, you must be able to trace the failure back to a *single* undercooked skill. If you layered everything too early, failure becomes un-diagnosable. Layer gradually; fuse only at the end.
